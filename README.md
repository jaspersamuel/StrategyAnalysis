# Strategy Analysis
Collaborated on a strategic analysis of Sony Corporation, examining declining PlayStation console sales and competitive pressures from mobile gaming, PC gaming, and emerging AR/VR technologies. Applied SWOT analysis, PESTLE analysis, and Porter’s Five Forces to evaluate Sony’s competitive position, industry dynamics, and business and HR strategies. Developed recommendations focused on expanding PlayStation into subscription-based gaming services, improving VR accessibility, diversifying revenue streams, and strengthening market competitiveness. The project also explored employee engagement, talent acquisition, and talent development, strengthening my understanding of strategic management, industry research, competitive analysis, and evidence-based business decision-making.
